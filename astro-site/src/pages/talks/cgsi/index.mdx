<Slide id="mathematical-model">

<h2>Mathematical model</h2>

The central object is the continuous-time covariance balance

$$
A\Sigma + \Sigma A^\top + Q = 0.
$$

If the drift admits a stable eigensystem, then

$$
\exp(tA) = R \exp(tB) R^{-1},
$$

which turns repeated propagation into a structured computational problem.

</Slide>
