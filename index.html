About me

I am a fourth year PhD candidate in the department of Biostatistics at University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), where I work under the supervision of Professor Marc Suchard. My research develops Bayesian and computational methods to study evolutionary and population-level dynamics. By modeling patterns of genetic diversity, migration, and selection, I seek to uncover how populations evolve across space and time and to identify the forces shaping these processes.
